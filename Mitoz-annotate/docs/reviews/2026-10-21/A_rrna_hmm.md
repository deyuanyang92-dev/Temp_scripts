## Declined to judge

- Whether the thin models (Onychophora n=3, Brachiopoda/Hemichordata n=4, Chaetognatha 7, Tardigrada 8, Xenacoelomorpha 10; MIN_REFS=3) are specific enough: the dev experiment gives sensitivity only (standalone HMM, leave-genus-out: Onychophora 1/4, Brachiopoda 4/8, Rotifera 1/2, Xenacoelomorpha 2/4, Tardigrada 3/4); false-positive behaviour on real mitochondrial sequence is unmeasured. Only a synthetic control was run: 41 models x 40 i.i.d. random 16-kb sequences (GC 25 % and 40 %) = 1640 searches with the production nhmmer settings gave 0 hits at E <= 1e-3, so E=1e-3 is not leaky on random sequence, but this says nothing about real CDS, tRNA or control-region sequence.
- Whether other phyla besides Placozoa contain split or duplicated rRNA references: only Placozoa (all 16 records) and Porifera (120 records) were fetched and counted; the other 27 phylum libraries were not re-derived from NCBI (would need ~1,600 records).
- End accuracy of HMM-added rRNAs after mito_curate R1 (outside scope; the dev A/B shows HMM-added ends are often 100s of nt off, curate only trims overlaps and fills gaps <= 300 nt).
- HMMER 3.1b2 versus 3.4 on divergent real data (both parsed the shipped models and gave the same tblout layout; no real-genome comparison).
- Whether table2asn accepts the /note text on rRNA features (not run; ncbi_validate was only read).
- The truth of the NCBI rRNA annotations used as references or benchmark truth (e.g. whether Placozoa "region a/b" pieces are annotated correctly in the source records).
- Anything in MitoZ's other scripts beyond the two uses of CDS_length_list (annotation.py:448 -> find_contro_region.py); findmitoscaf is not used by this pipeline as far as I read.
- Pre-existing mypy errors in mito_env.py:239-246 (doctor(): variable `p` reused as str and Path) are not part of this branch's diff; mito_rrna_hmm.py itself is clean under ruff and mypy.
