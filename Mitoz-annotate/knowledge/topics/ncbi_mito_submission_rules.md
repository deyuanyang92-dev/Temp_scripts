# NCBI/INSDC rules for mitochondrial genome records and how RefSeq (NC_) records are made

Scope: what GenBank requires and checks for a metazoan mitochondrial genome (feature keys, names, partial ends, introns, circular topology), which submission routes exist, what table2asn and the discrepancy report flag, and how RefSeq mitochondrial records relate to the INSDC records they are derived from (status words REVIEWED and PROVISIONAL). NCBI pages and GenBank flat files were read on 2026-10-20; observed record details come from efetch downloads named in each bullet.

## Submission routes and entry pages
- NCBI GenBank home (https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/genbank/) links the Submission Portal, table2asn, the Organelle Submission Guide, the Feature Table (INSDC) and Common Submission Errors pages (https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/genbank/)
- Mitochondrial guide: "Submitting Mitochondrial and Chloroplast Genomes to GenBank" (page footer says last updated 2026-05-04) (https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/genbank/organelle_submit/)
- Route in the guide: Submission Portal GenBank (SP-GenBank) > Eukaryote > "any other feature (coding regions, organelle genomes, regulatory regions, etc.)"; Sequences tab: genomic DNA, uploaded sequences from "mitochondrial or chloroplast", then "Are these complete mitochondrial genomes?" and, if yes, circular or linear (https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/genbank/organelle_submit/)
- The Genome Submission Portal is to be used for an organelle genome only when it accompanies a nuclear genome submission (https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/genbank/organelle_submit/)
- Annotation is required: records without it, or with inaccurate annotation, get delayed accessions and curators ask for resubmission; the record needs manual processing before release (https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/genbank/organelle_submit/)
- BankIt: the Submission Portal page states that SP-GenBank has expanded to accept most types historically submitted in BankIt, that BankIt will be discontinued in late 2026, and that only alignment import with feature propagation still needs BankIt (https://submit.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/about/bankit/)
- Old BankIt requirements page: sequence at least 200 nt; topology linear or circular where "circular must be complete"; location organelle (mitochondrion, chloroplast); features such as CDS, gene, rRNA, tRNA, exon, intron are added by upload or forms (https://ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/WebSub/html/requirements.html)
- table2asn is the command-line route for large submissions; the files must be emailed to GenBank, they are not uploaded through the portal (https://submit.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/about/bankit/)
- table2asn inputs: nucleotide FASTA with SeqID and organism, optional five-column .tbl starting `>Features <SeqID>`, template .sbt; defline modifiers `[topology=circular]`, `[location=mitochondrion]`, `[gcode=4]`; flags `-V v` validate, `-Z` discrepancy report (https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/genbank/table2asn/)

## Five-column feature table (what NCBI says to prepare)
- Columns 1-2 hold locations (for a complement-strand feature the larger coordinate goes first), column 3 the feature key, columns 4-5 qualifier and value; each sequence block starts with `>Feature <ID>` matching the FASTA ID; locations must be those of the genome as submitted (https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/genbank/organelle_submit/)
- codon_start defaults to 1 when omitted; do not supply protein_id values, they are assigned at processing (https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/genbank/organelle_submit/)
- SP-GenBank translates every CDS with the proper genetic code on upload and reports problems such as internal stop codons at the top of the page (https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/genbank/organelle_submit/)
- Check that every gene feature has a CDS, tRNA or rRNA; a gene must not be linked to a misc_feature; a protein-coding gene without CDS usually means internal stops or a missing start/stop in the translation; an essential gene written as misc_feature or pseudo can result from internal stops or from RNA editing (https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/genbank/organelle_submit/)
- Remove from tool output: misc_feature lines naming the annotation program, "label" terms, locus tags such as "gene 1", standard_name, BLAT output; when copying from a similar record drop locus_tag, gene_xref, GeneID and protein_id (https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/genbank/organelle_submit/)
- Product names must not begin with "TPA:", contain "No product string in profile", the word "partial" or a bracketed term (https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/genbank/organelle_submit/)
- Starts and ends should be checked with BLASTP and alignments of related proteins; many partial features in a tool's output signal that the tool could not find the real endpoints (https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/genbank/organelle_submit/)
- Genome-level guide: CDS and RNAs such as tRNAs and rRNAs "must have a corresponding gene feature"; /gene is reserved for the real symbol (e.g. trnG) and a tRNA label such as tRNA-Gly1 goes in /note (https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/genbank/eukaryotic_genome_submission_annotation/)

## Metazoan mitochondria checklist (NCBI)
- Each genome should have 13 CDS, 22 tRNA and 2 rRNA; known exceptions are Cnidaria and Porifera; if a feature is missing write to gb-admin with the SUB number (https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/genbank/organelle_submit/)
- ND3 of some Testudines/Aves carries an extra base that is skipped over; annotate it with a join in the span and the exception `ribosomal_slippage` (https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/genbank/organelle_submit/)
- A CDS rarely overlaps a tRNA by more than a few nucleotides; these coding regions use the mitochondrial TAA stop; features are on both strands, with few exceptions such as mollusks (https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/genbank/organelle_submit/)
- Fungal checklist item: intronic ORFs should lie within an intron; the animal list says nothing about group I/II introns, tRNA introns, trans-splicing or editing (https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/genbank/organelle_submit/) (knowledge/tools/table2asn.md:58-59)

## Feature keys, gene names and products
- gene: "region of biological interest identified as a gene and for which a name has been assigned"; CDS: "coding sequence ... (location includes stop codon)", must carry the conceptual translation unless pseudo, pseudogene, exception or transl_except is present (https://www.insdc.org/submitting-standards/feature-table/)
- rRNA: "mature ribosomal RNA", /product is the name qualifier; tRNA: "mature transfer RNA ... 50-150 bases long" with optional /anticodon and /product; NCBI validator wants rRNA names such as "16S ribosomal RNA" (https://www.insdc.org/submitting-standards/feature-table/) (https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/genbank/genome_validation/)
- D-loop: "displacement loop; a region within mitochondrial DNA in which a short stretch of RNA is paired with one strand of DNA"; rep_origin: origin of replication, /direction RIGHT, LEFT or BOTH; misc_feature: region that no other key can describe; intron: at least 10 nt (https://www.insdc.org/submitting-standards/feature-table/)
- /organelle takes the controlled value `mitochondrion` (also `mitochondrion:kinetoplast`) (https://www.insdc.org/submitting-standards/feature-table/)
- Control region in RefSeq records has no single key: human NC_012920.1 `D-loop complement(join(16024..16569,1..576))`; mouse NC_005089.1 `D-loop 15423..16299` plus `rep_origin 5160..5191` (note OL, /direction=right); Drosophila NC_024511.2 misc_feature 14917..19524 "A+T region; control region"; Apis NC_001566.1 misc_feature "A+T rich region"; Montipora NC_006902.1 misc_feature "putative control region" (https://eutils.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/entrez/eutils/efetch.fcgi?db=nuccore&id=NC_012920.1&rettype=gb&retmode=text) (https://eutils.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/entrez/eutils/efetch.fcgi?db=nuccore&id=NC_005089.1&rettype=gb&retmode=text) (https://eutils.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/entrez/eutils/efetch.fcgi?db=nuccore&id=NC_024511.2&rettype=gb&retmode=text)
- Human NC_012920.1 has no rep_origin and one extra `misc_feature 3107` with note "preserves historical genome annotation numbering" (position 3107 is an N placeholder in the sequence) (https://eutils.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/entrez/eutils/efetch.fcgi?db=nuccore&id=NC_012920.1&rettype=gb&retmode=text)
- rRNA product strings vary: human `s-rRNA` / `l-rRNA` (notes 12S / 16S), C. elegans and Metridium `s-rRNA` / `l-rRNA`, Drosophila `12S ribosomal RNA` / `16S ribosomal RNA` with gene `srRNA` / `lrRNA`; the benchmark name map covers s-rrna, l-rrna and the 12S/16S strings (https://eutils.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/entrez/eutils/efetch.fcgi?db=nuccore&id=NC_012920.1&rettype=gb&retmode=text) (https://eutils.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/entrez/eutils/efetch.fcgi?db=nuccore&id=NC_024511.2&rettype=gb&retmode=text) (benchmark/mito_names.py:8-10)
- Protein products and symbols: human CDS use gene `ND1`, `COX1`, product `NADH dehydrogenase subunit 1`, `cytochrome c oxidase subunit I`; rRNA and tRNA features in the same record have the symbols RNR1/RNR2 and TRNF etc. (HGNC); other records use ND1/COX1/CYTB without HGNC style (https://eutils.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/entrez/eutils/efetch.fcgi?db=nuccore&id=NC_012920.1&rettype=gb&retmode=text) (https://eutils.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/entrez/eutils/efetch.fcgi?db=nuccore&id=NC_000933.1&rettype=gb&retmode=text)
- Gene counts in observed RefSeq records: human 37 genes (13 CDS, 22 tRNA, 2 rRNA); C. elegans 36 genes (12 CDS: no ATP8; 22 tRNA; 2 rRNA); Metridium 14 CDS (13 genes plus an ORF in an intron), 2 tRNA, 2 rRNA; Mnemiopsis 11 CDS, 2 rRNA, no tRNA (https://eutils.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/entrez/eutils/efetch.fcgi?db=nuccore&id=NC_001328.1&rettype=gb&retmode=text) (https://eutils.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/entrez/eutils/efetch.fcgi?db=nuccore&id=NC_000933.1&rettype=gb&retmode=text) (https://eutils.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/entrez/eutils/efetch.fcgi?db=nuccore&id=NC_016117.1&rettype=gb&retmode=text)

## Partial genes, stops and exceptions
- Partial ends use `<` and `>` in the location; `/partial` is not for new entries since 2001-12-15 (https://www.insdc.org/submitting-standards/feature-table/)
- Drosophila NC_024511.2 writes COX1 as `<1474..3009` with note "start codon not determined" (https://eutils.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/entrez/eutils/efetch.fcgi?db=nuccore&id=NC_024511.2&rettype=gb&retmode=text)
- Incomplete stop codon completed by polyadenylation: `/transl_except=(pos:<last base(s)>,aa:TERM)` plus note "stop codon completed by the addition of 3' A residues to the mRNA"; do not use /exception where transl_except suffices (https://www.insdc.org/submitting-standards/feature-table/)
- /ribosomal_slippage must not be combined with /exception; use a join in the location (https://www.insdc.org/submitting-standards/feature-table/)
- SEQ_FEAT.NoStop: CDS not partial at 3' and not ending in a stop; SEQ_FEAT.StartCodon: not partial at 5' and no start codon; SEQ_FEAT.InternalStop with SEQ_INST.StopInProtein; SEQ_FEAT.GenCodeInvalid/GenCodeMismatch (for non-prokaryotes NCBI says the genetic-code error can be ignored and will be addressed in processing) (https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/genbank/genome_validation/)
- SEQ_FEAT.MissingTrnaAA (tRNA without amino acid in product), SEQ_FEAT.ShortIntron (intron below 11 bp), SEQ_FEAT.PartialProblem (partial end not at sequence end or intron/exon boundary), SEQ_INST.TerminalNs (remove terminal Ns or indicate circular) (https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/genbank/genome_validation/)
- Older list: SEQ_FEAT_InternalStop and SEQ_FEAT_PartialProblemHasStop (internal stop in a partial CDS), DISC_FEATURE_MOLTYPE_MISMATCH (rRNA features need genomic DNA molecule type), DISC_NO_ANNOTATION (https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/genbank/validation/)
- Tested locally with table2asn 1.28.1094: T/TA-ended CDS with only the note get SEQ_FEAT.NoStop, adding correct transl_except clears it; mgcode missing gives GenCodeMismatch; CTG start fails under code 5 (knowledge/tools/table2asn.md:26-31)
- table2asn does not judge biology: a CDS that starts and ends on valid codons passes even with a wrong boundary (knowledge/tools/table2asn.md:49)

## Discrepancy report (table2asn -Z)
- The report is "something of a blunt instrument that reports everything that fails its tests" and does not decide whether a failure is real or reflects the biology; FATAL categories should almost always be fixed before submission (https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/genbank/asndisc/)
- FATAL categories relevant to mitogenomes include MISSING_GENES, PARTIAL_PROBLEMS, PSEUDO_MISMATCH, RIBOSOMAL_SLIPPAGE, SHORT_RRNA, OVERLAPPING_RRNAS, RRNA_NAME_CONFLICTS, SUSPECT_PRODUCT_NAMES, CONTAINED_CDS and RNA_CDS_OVERLAP (when coding regions are completely contained in RNAs) (https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/genbank/asndisc/)
- OVERLAPPING_CDS, CONTAINED_CDS and RNA_CDS_OVERLAP are described as categories that find CDS and/or RNAs overlapping or contained within each other, "suspicious" but sometimes valid and needing confirmation by the submitter; whether the 46-nt ATP8/ATP6 overlap of human mtDNA triggers OVERLAPPING_CDS was not tested here (未确证) (https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/genbank/asndisc/)
- MitoZ runs tbl2asn with `-V vb` only, without `-M n` or the discrepancy report (knowledge/tools/table2asn.md:43)

## Circular topology and features across the origin
- Declare circularity: `[topology=circular]` in the table2asn defline, the SP-GenBank "circular or linear" question, and in BankIt only complete molecules may be circular (https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/genbank/table2asn/) (https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/genbank/organelle_submit/) (https://ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/WebSub/html/requirements.html)
- INSDC location syntax: `join(l1,l2,...)` = elements placed end-to-end into one contiguous sequence; `complement(join(...))` complements the joined segments; a site between bases of a circular molecule of length n is written `n^1` (https://www.insdc.org/submitting-standards/feature-table/)
- Origin-spanning example in RefSeq: human D-loop `complement(join(16024..16569,1..576))` in NC_012920.1 (16569 bp) and `complement(join(16029..16571,1..579))` in the older NC_001807.4; no CDS or tRNA of the records fetched crosses the origin (https://eutils.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/entrez/eutils/efetch.fcgi?db=nuccore&id=NC_012920.1&rettype=gb&retmode=text) (https://eutils.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/entrez/eutils/efetch.fcgi?db=nuccore&id=NC_001807.4&rettype=gb&retmode=text)
- The sequence start differs by record: in NC_012920.1 position 1 lies inside the control region, 576 bp before TRNF (577..647), while chimpanzee NC_001643.1 has its D-loop as a plain `15442..16554` ending at the sequence end; compare benchmark and RefSeq coordinates after rotating to a common start (https://eutils.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/entrez/eutils/efetch.fcgi?db=nuccore&id=NC_012920.1&rettype=gb&retmode=text) (https://eutils.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/entrez/eutils/efetch.fcgi?db=nuccore&id=NC_001643.1&rettype=gb&retmode=text)
- MitoAnnotator output refers to sequences circularly shifted to begin with the trnF locus (doi:10.1093/nar/gkz833)
- MitoZ treats every sequence as linear for CDS/tRNA/rRNA search, so genes across the origin lose start or stop; remedy is to re-break the circle in a gene-free region (knowledge/tools/mitoz.md:41) (knowledge/tools/mitoz.md:57)
- table2asn gives SEQ_INST.CompleteCircleProblem as a harmless warning for circular records (knowledge/tools/table2asn.md:35)

## Introns (group I/II)
- INSDC has an `intron` key (at least 10 nt, qualifiers /gene, /number, /trans_splicing) and no qualifier for intron group; the CDS is written as a join of exons (https://www.insdc.org/submitting-standards/feature-table/)
- Montipora cactus NC_006902.1 (REVIEWED): `intron 3161..14645 /gene="ND5" /note="group I intron"` and `CDS join(2440..3159,14645..15760)`; the CDS gap is 3160..14644, so the intron feature is shifted by one base compared with the join (3161..14645); Montipora efflorescens NC_040137.1 is consistent: `join(2433..3152,14644..15759)` with `intron 3153..14643` (https://eutils.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/entrez/eutils/efetch.fcgi?db=nuccore&id=NC_006902.1&rettype=gb&retmode=text) (https://eutils.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/entrez/eutils/efetch.fcgi?db=nuccore&id=NC_040137.1&rettype=gb&retmode=text)
- Metridium senile NC_000933.1: `CDS join(3263..4155,5009..5708)` with `intron 4156..5008 /gene="COX1"`, and `CDS join(7027..7743,9425..10510)` with `intron 7744..9424 /gene="ND5"`; the intron ORF is a separate CDS `4310..4981` with `/product="homing endonuclease"` and note "ORF found in the intron of the COI gene"; no exon features are used (https://eutils.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/entrez/eutils/efetch.fcgi?db=nuccore&id=NC_000933.1&rettype=gb&retmode=text)
- table2asn 1.28.1094 did not enforce splice-site consensus for a two-exon mitochondrial CDS in one test, so a clean validation says nothing about intron boundaries (one test only) (knowledge/tools/table2asn.md:37)
- SEQ_FEAT.ShortIntron flags a CDS intron shorter than 11 bp, usually inserted to patch a frameshift (https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/genbank/genome_validation/)

## How RefSeq mitochondrial records (NC_) are made
- RefSeq is a non-redundant set of reference standards derived from the INSDC databases and includes organelle genomes; genomic RefSeq records for organelles are created when the sequence is submitted to the INSDC; the Entrez Genomes flow supplies all bacterial, viral, organelle and plasmid RefSeq records; organelles that are not part of a whole-genome submission are on the RefSeq FTP site (https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/sites/books/NBK50679/)
- Complete organelle genomes are one of the criteria for species inclusion in RefSeq; RefSeq records are maintained by NCBI and may differ from the INSD record "in small details to conform to RefSeq feature annotation standards"; NCBI staff curate loci on request or by protein-homology-based curation (doi:10.1093/nar/gkn721)
- Targeted annotation for organelle and microbial genomes: tRNAs added with tRNAscan-SE, 5S with Infernal, 16S/23S from an internal database; corrections reported together for microbial and organelle records: +751 rRNA, +3679 tRNA, -4 rRNA, -47 tRNA, 103 rRNA and 115 tRNA strand corrections (doi:10.1093/nar/gkn721)
- The record's COMMENT block carries the status and source: "PROVISIONAL REFSEQ: This record has not yet been subject to final NCBI review. The reference sequence was derived from <INSDC accession>" or "REVIEWED REFSEQ: This record has been curated by NCBI staff. The reference sequence was derived from <INSDC accession>" (https://eutils.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/entrez/eutils/efetch.fcgi?db=nuccore&id=NC_012920.1&rettype=gb&retmode=text) (https://eutils.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/entrez/eutils/efetch.fcgi?db=nuccore&id=NC_000933.1&rettype=gb&retmode=text)
- Meaning from RefSeq documentation: initial records have PROVISIONAL, PREDICTED or INFERRED status; manual sequence-level review gives VALIDATED; additional annotation and summary give REVIEWED; PROVISIONAL records represent one INSDC source sequence and may be updated automatically to a longer INSDC sequence, while VALIDATED/REVIEWED records may be built from several INSDC sequences (https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/refseq/about/) (https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/sites/books/NBK50679/)
- REVIEWED records observed: Metridium NC_000933.1 (from AF000023), Montipora cactus NC_006902.1, Montipora efflorescens NC_040137.1, sea urchin NC_001453.1 (from X12631), Mytilus NC_006161.1 (from AY484747), chimpanzee NC_001643.1, mouse NC_005089.1, Amphimedon NC_008944.1, Mnemiopsis NC_016117.1 (https://eutils.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/entrez/eutils/efetch.fcgi?db=nuccore&id=NC_001453.1&rettype=gb&retmode=text) (https://eutils.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/entrez/eutils/efetch.fcgi?db=nuccore&id=NC_006161.1&rettype=gb&retmode=text)
- PROVISIONAL records observed: human NC_012920.1 (from J01415; "a corrected version of the HUMMTCG reference sequence"), Drosophila NC_024511.2 (identical to KJ947872), C. elegans NC_001328.1 (from X54252), Apis NC_001566.1 (from L06178), Platynereis NC_000931.1; so status does not track how important or well annotated a genome is (https://eutils.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/entrez/eutils/efetch.fcgi?db=nuccore&id=NC_012920.1&rettype=gb&retmode=text) (https://eutils.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/entrez/eutils/efetch.fcgi?db=nuccore&id=NC_024511.2&rettype=gb&retmode=text) (https://eutils.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/entrez/eutils/efetch.fcgi?db=nuccore&id=NC_001328.1&rettype=gb&retmode=text)
- Version and batch effects: 13 of the 15 records fetched carry DBLINK BioProject PRJNA927338 (the exceptions are Mytilus NC_006161.1 and the older human NC_001807.4); 12 of them have LOCUS date 03-APR-2023 and Montipora efflorescens NC_040137.1 has 28-JUL-2026; locus_tag KEF* appears in Drosophila, C. elegans, Apis, sea urchin and chimpanzee but not in human NC_012920.1, mouse, Metridium or Platynereis; what that 2023 update did is 未确证 (https://eutils.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/entrez/eutils/efetch.fcgi?db=nuccore&id=NC_001566.1&rettype=gb&retmode=text) (https://eutils.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/entrez/eutils/efetch.fcgi?db=nuccore&id=NC_005089.1&rettype=gb&retmode=text)
- RefSeq versions get replaced: NC_024511.2 replaced NC_024511.1 on 2014-08-01 and NC_012920.1 replaced AC_000021.2 on 2009-07-08, so a benchmark must record the RefSeq version it used (https://eutils.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/entrez/eutils/efetch.fcgi?db=nuccore&id=NC_024511.2&rettype=gb&retmode=text) (https://eutils.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/entrez/eutils/efetch.fcgi?db=nuccore&id=NC_012920.1&rettype=gb&retmode=text)
- Process flows named by NCBI: collaboration, annotation by NCBI, and propagation of GenBank annotation with targeted curation; for Drosophila melanogaster annotation updates go to the INSDC and are propagated to RefSeq, consistent with NC_024511.2 being "identical to KJ947872" and citing a Berkeley Drosophila Genome Project direct submission as REFERENCE 2 (doi:10.1093/nar/gkn721) (https://eutils.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/entrez/eutils/efetch.fcgi?db=nuccore&id=NC_024511.2&rettype=gb&retmode=text)
- Error reports go through the Gene and RefSeq Feedback form; genome-sequence errors may be reported to the original sequencing group (https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/sites/books/NBK50679/)

## RefSeq mitogenome annotation is not a clean gold standard
- RefSeq and GenBank are the major sources of annotated mitogenomes and their consistency and quality problems "have been described repeatedly"; incomplete stops are not annotated consistently in RefSeq (doi:10.1093/nar/gkz833)
- Manual check of 16 echinoderm mitogenomes: more than a dozen RefSeq errors (missing tRNAs, inaccurate positions, wrong reading directions, incorrect anticodons and isoacceptor families) in 7 of the 16 (doi:10.1093/nar/gkr1131)
- RefSeq tRNA annotation shows "a high level of misannotations" of the Ser1/Ser2 and Leu1/Leu2 type (doi:10.1093/nar/gkr1131)
- Preprint re-annotation of Drosophila: RefSeq NC_024511.2 has five tRNAs too short to fold, an ND1 start of ATA where TTG is predicted, and the control region starting 2 bp late; NC_005781.1 (D. simulans RefSeq) lacks the control region; GenBank CM016414.1 has a duplicated 201 bp of COII and a 1-bp deletion in a poly-A run of ND1 (doi:10.1101/2021.06.05.447193)
- Practical rule for the benchmark: treat RefSeq mismatches as candidates for either a pipeline error or a RefSeq error, and record status (REVIEWED/PROVISIONAL), version and derived-from accession per reference record (https://eutils.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/entrez/eutils/efetch.fcgi?db=nuccore&id=NC_024511.2&rettype=gb&retmode=text) (https://eutils.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/entrez/eutils/efetch.fcgi?db=nuccore&id=NC_000933.1&rettype=gb&retmode=text)
