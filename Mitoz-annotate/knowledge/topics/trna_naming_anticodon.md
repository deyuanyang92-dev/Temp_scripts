# Mitochondrial tRNA naming, anticodons, missing and armless tRNAs

Scope: how metazoan mitochondrial tRNA genes are named in INSDC/RefSeq and in annotation tools, what the /anticodon qualifier looks like, which taxa lack tRNAs or have truncated/armless tRNAs, and what this means for matching a benchmark annotation to RefSeq. Record facts (coordinates, qualifiers, lengths) were read from the efetch GenBank flat files named in each bullet on 2026-10-20.

## INSDC and NCBI rules for tRNA features
- The tRNA feature key is "mature transfer RNA, a small RNA molecule (limited to 50-150 bases long)"; /anticodon, /gene, /product, /note, /pseudo are optional qualifiers (https://www.insdc.org/submitting-standards/feature-table/)
- /anticodon syntax: `(pos:<location>,aa:<amino_acid>,seq:<text>)`; examples `(pos:34..36,aa:Phe,seq:aaa)`, `(pos:join(5,495..496),aa:Leu,seq:taa)`, `(pos:complement(4156..4158),aa:Gln,seq:ttg)` (https://www.insdc.org/submitting-standards/feature-table/)
- INSDC worked example of a Leu(UUR) tRNA: `/gene="tRNA-Leu(UUR)"`, `/anticodon=(pos:678..680,aa:Leu,seq:taa)`, `/product="transfer RNA-Leu(UUR)"` (a Yersinia sequence, used only to show the syntax) (https://www.insdc.org/submitting-standards/feature-table/)
- NCBI eukaryotic annotation guide: /gene is reserved for the real gene symbol such as "trnG"; a label like "tRNA-Gly1" belongs in /note, not /gene; unknown amino acid is written `tRNA-Xxx`; a tRNA pseudogene gets /pseudo; every tRNA/rRNA/CDS needs a corresponding gene feature (https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/genbank/eukaryotic_genome_submission_annotation/)
- Validator: SEQ_FEAT.MissingTrnaAA warns when the product does not name the amino acid; NCBI says to use `tRNA-Xxx` if unknown (https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/genbank/genome_validation/)
- Metazoan checklist from NCBI: 13 CDS, 22 tRNA, 2 rRNA with known exceptions Cnidaria and Porifera; a missing expected feature should be reported to gb-admin with the SUB number; features are on both strands except in a few taxa such as mollusks (https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/genbank/organelle_submit/)
- /codon_recognized (UUR, CUN, AGN, AGY, UCN) occurs in many RefSeq mitochondrial tRNA features but is not defined on the INSDC feature-table page fetched here (0 hits for the word on that page) (https://www.insdc.org/submitting-standards/feature-table/)

## Leu and Ser: the numbering trap (L1/L2, S1/S2)
- Human RefSeq NC_012920.1 (HGNC symbols): TRNL1 3230..3304 anticodon taa, codon_recognized UUR ("tRNA leucine 1 (UUA/G)"); TRNL2 12266..12336 anticodon tag, CUN; TRNS1 complement(7446..7514) anticodon tga, UCN; TRNS2 12207..12265 anticodon gct, AGY ("tRNA serine 2 (AGU/C)") (https://eutils.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/entrez/eutils/efetch.fcgi?db=nuccore&id=NC_012920.1&rettype=gb&retmode=text)
- Mouse RefSeq NC_005089.1 uses the same scheme: TrnL1 UUR, TrnL2 CUN, TrnS1 UCN, TrnS2 AGY with anticodon gct (https://eutils.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/entrez/eutils/efetch.fcgi?db=nuccore&id=NC_005089.1&rettype=gb&retmode=text)
- Drosophila RefSeq NC_024511.2 uses the same scheme although it is an invertebrate: trnL1 (gene_synonym mt:tRNA:Leu-TAA, 3012..3077), trnL2 (Leu-TAG), trnS1 (Ser-TGA, 11638..11703), trnS2 (Ser-GCT, 6191..6258); no /anticodon qualifier on any tRNA (https://eutils.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/entrez/eutils/efetch.fcgi?db=nuccore&id=NC_024511.2&rettype=gb&retmode=text)
- The MITOS/MiTFi convention defines L1/L2 and S1/S2 by codon family as CTN/TTR and AGN/TCN, i.e. L1 = Leu CUN (anticodon TAG), L2 = Leu UUR (TAA), S1 = Ser AGN, S2 = Ser UCN (TGA), the opposite of the human/mouse/Drosophila RefSeq numbering above (knowledge/tools/mitos2.md:39)
- The MITOS maintainers' tracker already notes that L1/L2 and S1/S2 numbering is used differently for human/primates and for the rest, and a user proposes dropping the numbers for the anticodon (open since 2020) (knowledge/tools/mitos2.md:64)
- Newer-style RefSeq records carry no number at all: product `tRNA-Leu` or `tRNA-Ser` plus `/codon_recognized`: C. elegans NC_001328.1 (Leu UUR, Leu CUN, Ser AGN, Ser UCN), Apis NC_001566.1 (Ser AGN, Leu UUR, Ser UCN, Leu CUN), sea urchin NC_001453.1, Platynereis NC_000931.1; chimpanzee NC_001643.1 and the sponge Amphimedon NC_008944.1 write AGY instead of AGN (https://eutils.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/entrez/eutils/efetch.fcgi?db=nuccore&id=NC_001328.1&rettype=gb&retmode=text) (https://eutils.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/entrez/eutils/efetch.fcgi?db=nuccore&id=NC_001566.1&rettype=gb&retmode=text) (https://eutils.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/entrez/eutils/efetch.fcgi?db=nuccore&id=NC_001643.1&rettype=gb&retmode=text) (https://eutils.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/entrez/eutils/efetch.fcgi?db=nuccore&id=NC_008944.1&rettype=gb&retmode=text)
- Some records carry no discriminator: Mytilus edulis NC_006161.1 has two features simply named `tRNA-Ser` without codon_recognized or anticodon (and 23 tRNA features in total, one more than the usual 22) (https://eutils.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/entrez/eutils/efetch.fcgi?db=nuccore&id=NC_006161.1&rettype=gb&retmode=text)
- Consequence for a benchmark: never match tRNAs by the L1/L2/S1/S2 index; match by (amino acid, codon family or anticodon) and then by overlap of coordinates, and treat AGN and AGY as the same Ser family (https://eutils.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/entrez/eutils/efetch.fcgi?db=nuccore&id=NC_012920.1&rettype=gb&retmode=text) (https://eutils.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/entrez/eutils/efetch.fcgi?db=nuccore&id=NC_001566.1&rettype=gb&retmode=text) (knowledge/tools/mitos2.md:39)
- Anticodons seen for the four families in RefSeq: Leu UUR taa, Leu CUN tag, Ser UCN tga, Ser AGN/AGY gct; a Ser AGN anticodon tct is 未确证 (not seen in any record fetched; the tRNA-Ser AGR/AGN anticodon change is discussed in the arthropod AGG study, doi:10.1371/journal.pbio.0040127)
- Orthology of the two Leu genes cannot be fixed from the anticodon alone because remolded Leu-UUR genes have replaced Leu-CUN genes several times in metazoan evolution; for serine the two groups differ strongly and are told apart by codons (UCN versus AGY) (doi:10.1093/nar/gkr1131)
- In Jühling et al. the D-domain-less serine tRNA is called "Ser1" (lacking the D-domain in nearly all Metazoa) and the adjacent same-strand pair "tRNASer1-tRNALeu1" occurs in 230 vertebrates; the table gives a 1-nt overlap, and in mouse NC_005089.1 TrnS2 (AGY) 11613..11671 and TrnL2 (CUN) 11671..11741 are same-strand neighbours overlapping by 1 nt, which suggests their Ser1 = AGY and Leu1 = CUN, i.e. the invertebrate scheme (inference from adjacency only, 未确证) (doi:10.1093/nar/gkr1131) (https://eutils.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/entrez/eutils/efetch.fcgi?db=nuccore&id=NC_005089.1&rettype=gb&retmode=text)

## How completely RefSeq records give anticodons
- /anticodon is present on all 22 tRNAs of human NC_012920.1, on 12 tRNAs of mouse NC_005089.1, and on none of the tRNAs of Drosophila NC_024511.2, C. elegans NC_001328.1, Apis NC_001566.1, Mytilus NC_006161.1, sea urchin NC_001453.1, Platynereis NC_000931.1, chimpanzee NC_001643.1, Metridium NC_000933.1, Montipora NC_006902.1 (counts of the string "anticodon" in the flat files) (https://eutils.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/entrez/eutils/efetch.fcgi?db=nuccore&id=NC_012920.1&rettype=gb&retmode=text) (https://eutils.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/entrez/eutils/efetch.fcgi?db=nuccore&id=NC_005089.1&rettype=gb&retmode=text)
- Where /anticodon is absent the information sits in /gene_synonym (Drosophila `mt:tRNA:Leu-TAA`), in /note (`mt:tRNA:Ile-GAT-RA`), in /codon_recognized (Leu/Ser only), or nowhere; the anticodon then has to be read from the sequence (https://eutils.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/entrez/eutils/efetch.fcgi?db=nuccore&id=NC_024511.2&rettype=gb&retmode=text)
- Gene-symbol spelling for the same Leu gene differs by record: `TRNL1` (human), `TrnL1` (mouse), `trnL1` (Drosophila), none (C. elegans, Apis); product text is `tRNA-Leu` in all of them (https://eutils.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/entrez/eutils/efetch.fcgi?db=nuccore&id=NC_012920.1&rettype=gb&retmode=text) (https://eutils.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/entrez/eutils/efetch.fcgi?db=nuccore&id=NC_005089.1&rettype=gb&retmode=text)
- Several newer-style records have no /gene on tRNAs at all, only locus_tag (KEF35_t07 etc.) and product (https://eutils.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/entrez/eutils/efetch.fcgi?db=nuccore&id=NC_001453.1&rettype=gb&retmode=text)
- The benchmark name parser reads amino acid from `product`/`gene` (tRNA-Xxx or trnX) and anticodon from /anticodon, then `(xxx)` in gene/product, then a note (benchmark/mito_names.py:40-54)

## How annotation tools name tRNAs (join keys)
- MiTFi (inside MitoZ and MITOS2) names the tRNA from the anticodon and the chosen genetic code, not from the covariance model that matched; a hit found by trnP.cm can be named trnY, and `trnX(---)` means no anticodon could be read (knowledge/tools/mitos2.md:38)
- MiTFi has 22 CM files Metazoa_{A,C,D,E,F,G,H,I,K,L1,L2,M,N,P,Q,R,S1,S2,T,V,W,Y}.cm; an example run labelled anticodon UAG as L1 from model Metazoa_L2.cm (knowledge/tools/mitfi.md:20-21)
- MITOS2 output name is `NAME[_COPY][-PART](anticodon)` in lower case, e.g. `trnM_0(cat)`, `(---)` when no anticodon (knowledge/tools/mitos2.md:40)
- MitoZ converts MiTFi output by deleting the digits 1/2 (L1 and L2 both become L, S1 and S2 become S), writes gene `trn<AA>(<anticodon>)` with lower-case RNA letters such as `trnL(uaa)` and product `tRNA-<Three-letter>`; downstream code keyed on trnL/trnS alone merges the copies (knowledge/tools/mitfi.md:26) (knowledge/tools/mitfi.md:44) (knowledge/tools/mitfi.md:56)
- ARWEN/ARAGORN print `mtRNA-???` with a 2- or 4-letter anticodon for 6- or 8-base anticodon loops, so the amino acid is unreadable and the pipeline parser drops such calls (knowledge/tools/arwen.md:28) (knowledge/tools/aragorn.md:32)
- tRNAscan-SE in organellar mode with the default table calls mitochondrial Trp(TCA) a suppressor ("Sup"); `-g` with a mitochondrial code table fixes it; the pipeline parser drops every Sup and Undet row (knowledge/tools/trnascan-se.md:44) (knowledge/tools/trnascan-se.md:61)
- Mitochondrial anticodon-to-amino-acid labels depend on the genetic code given to the tool, so the same code must be passed to MITOS2, ARWEN/ARAGORN and tRNAscan-SE (knowledge/tools/mitos2.md:87) (knowledge/tools/aragorn.md:65)

## tRNA genes missing in whole groups
- NCBI's own checklist lists Cnidaria and Porifera as exceptions to 22 tRNAs (https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/genbank/organelle_submit/)
- Cnidaria appear to encode only tRNA-Met and tRNA-Trp (Morrison, citing Beagley et al.) (https://arxiv.org/pdf/1001.3813)
- Cnidarians and some Ceractinomorpha sponges lost up to 21 tRNA genes and encode only tRNA-Met, some also tRNA-Trp or copies of tRNA-Met; marsupials lost a single tRNA-Lys gene (doi:10.1093/nar/gkr1131)
- Ctenophores lost all mt-tRNA genes, cnidarians retained at most two, placozoans encode a complete set; sponges range from two (demosponge Keratosa) to 27 (Oscarella); haplosclerid Clade B sponges range from 3 to 25 tRNA genes; in Keratosa the retained genes are trnW(uca) and trnM(cau)f, and some octocorals lost trnW but kept trnM (doi:10.1093/gbe/evaf020)
- Myxozoa lost all tRNA genes; Chaetognatha lost most of them (doi:10.1093/gbe/evaf020) (doi:10.1093/nar/gkr1131)
- Records: Metridium senile NC_000933.1 has tRNA-Met 1..71 and tRNA-Trp 10600..10669 only; Montipora cactus NC_006902.1 has tRNA-Met 1..71 and tRNA-Trp 15790..15859 only; Mnemiopsis leidyi NC_016117.1 has no tRNA feature; the sponge Amphimedon queenslandica NC_008944.1 has 17 tRNA features including two Met and two Arg and no Leu (https://eutils.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/entrez/eutils/efetch.fcgi?db=nuccore&id=NC_000933.1&rettype=gb&retmode=text) (https://eutils.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/entrez/eutils/efetch.fcgi?db=nuccore&id=NC_006902.1&rettype=gb&retmode=text) (https://eutils.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/entrez/eutils/efetch.fcgi?db=nuccore&id=NC_016117.1&rettype=gb&retmode=text) (https://eutils.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/entrez/eutils/efetch.fcgi?db=nuccore&id=NC_008944.1&rettype=gb&retmode=text)
- Tools that always report the best hit of each of the 22 families produce false positives in these clades: loss of tRNAs in Cnidaria accounts for about 283 false MiTFi predictions; a tRNA count below 22 should not be forced (doi:10.1093/nar/gkr1131) (knowledge/tools/mitfi.md:55)
- Local benchmark of MITOS2 fast mode: 96.7 % sensitivity and 21 false positives on 85 cnidarian/sponge/placozoan RefSeq mitogenomes (183 tRNAs) (knowledge/tools/mitos2.md:77)
- Some marsupial tRNAs get the wrong amino acid from the anticodon: tRNA-Asp with GCC anticodon is reported as a second tRNA-Gly by MitoZ and there is no option to change the expected anticodons (knowledge/tools/mitfi.md:42)

## Armless, truncated and unusual tRNAs
- More than 90 % of mt-tRNAs fold into the cloverleaf; frequent exceptions lack the D-domain or the T-domain (doi:10.1093/nar/gkr1131)
- The D-domain-less serine tRNA ("Ser1" there) is the well known case in nearly all Metazoa; the other serine ("Ser2") lacks the D-domain only in Lophotrochozoa and Ecdysozoa, most often in Bryozoa and Nematoda; tRNA-Cys lost the D-domain independently in Amphibia, Tunicata, Bryozoa, Platyhelminthes, Arthropoda, Lepidosauria, Mammalia (doi:10.1093/nar/gkr1131)
- Arm loss is concentrated in Chelicerata, Crustacea and Myriapoda; Insecta rarely deviate; T-domain loss is found in Ecdysozoa and Lophotrochozoa, and among deuterostomes only in Tunicata (doi:10.1093/nar/gkr1131)
- Arachnida: RefSeq contained 9191 annotated mt-tRNAs; ARWEN found 82.8 %, tRNAscan-SE 50.4 %, MiTFi 89.7 %; Cecidomyiidae tRNAs lack the 3' end; Sciaroidea tRNAs lack the T-stem region (doi:10.1093/nar/gkr1131)
- Scorpion example: tRNAscan-SE reported 78 candidates in Mesobuthus gibbosus, 20 of them correct, only 4 above the default COVE score of 20; lowering the cut-off to 0.1 gave random hits such as a tRNA-Asp that fitted no better than a random sequence (https://arxiv.org/pdf/1001.3813)
- Nematoda: "bizarre tRNAs appear to be the rule"; tRNAs without T-domain occur throughout the group, tRNA-Cys lacks either the D- or the T-domain as the rule (doi:10.1093/nar/gkr1131)
- Enoplea (Dorylaimia): nematode-specific models predicted extremely truncated tRNAs with only acceptor and anticodon stems for Asn, Cys and Tyr; the authors call this compelling evidence for functional tRNAs lacking both T- and D-domains, while Morrison's preprint presents D-/T-armless tRNAs in Dorylaimia only as a hypothesis supported by consistency among relatives (doi:10.1093/nar/gkr1131) (https://arxiv.org/pdf/1001.3813)
- tRNAscan-SE finds fewer than half of the nematode mt-tRNA genes even with its "Nematode mito" setting (https://arxiv.org/pdf/1001.3813)
- Feature lengths in RefSeq reflect this: C. elegans NC_001328.1 tRNAs are 53-63 nt (mean 56.3) against 59-75 nt (mean 68.5) in human NC_012920.1 and 61-73 nt in Drosophila NC_024511.2 (computed from the feature coordinates) (https://eutils.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/entrez/eutils/efetch.fcgi?db=nuccore&id=NC_001328.1&rettype=gb&retmode=text) (https://eutils.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/entrez/eutils/efetch.fcgi?db=nuccore&id=NC_012920.1&rettype=gb&retmode=text)
- Truncated acceptor stems (5' stem acting as template for editing of the 3' half) were proposed for arachnids and Pulmonata tRNA-Gly, and confirmed in structure for Lithobius forficatus and relatives (Myriapoda) (https://arxiv.org/pdf/1001.3813) (doi:10.1093/nar/gkr1131)
- Both large reviews state that RefSeq tRNA annotation is inconsistent: missed genes, wrong locations, "false complements" (wrong strand), inconsistent 5'/3' boundary criteria (Morrison); wrong reading direction and wrong anticodons in 7 of 16 echinoderm mitogenomes (Jühling) (https://arxiv.org/pdf/1001.3813) (doi:10.1093/nar/gkr1131)

## Overlapping tRNAs and tRNA editing
- Overlap of coding regions is usually considered restricted to under 5 nt except the well documented ATP6-ATP8 and ND4-ND4L overlaps; most long overlaps found in databases come from inconsistent annotation decisions rather than biology (https://arxiv.org/pdf/1001.3813)
- NCBI checklist: a CDS rarely overlaps a tRNA by more than a few nucleotides (https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/genbank/organelle_submit/)
- Overlap of tRNA genes is usually ascribed to post-transcriptional editing such as 3' polyadenylation, but this was shown experimentally only in few cases and for overlaps shorter than 7 nt (https://arxiv.org/pdf/1001.3813)
- Systematic count over 1876 RefSeq metazoan mitogenomes: more than 3700 overlaps between tRNA genes (overlaps up to 10 nt treated as distinct loci); single-nucleotide overlaps are the most common; taxonomically conserved overlaps are mostly between tRNAs on different strands, e.g. tRNA-Ile/tRNA-Gln in 1056 vertebrates and tRNA-Trp/tRNA-Cys in Arthropoda with up to 8 nt overlap (doi:10.1093/nar/gkr1131)
- In that comparison the authors disregarded strand and the Ser1/Ser2, Leu1/Leu2 distinction because the RefSeq annotation "shows a high level of misannotations of this type" (doi:10.1093/nar/gkr1131)
- A search for tRNAs encoded on the reverse strand of known tRNA genes (postulated by others) found no near-perfect opposite-strand overlaps (doi:10.1093/nar/gkr1131)
- Overlap examples from the same preprint: tRNA-Phe of Aphrocallistes vastus (Porifera) annotated inside NAD5 (removed by using a truncated TA instead of TAA as stop); tRNA-Asp of the scorpion Mesobuthus gibbosus annotated inside the s-rRNA; tRNA-Ser(UCN) of Steganacarus magnus overlapping the l-rRNA end; tRNA-Ser(UCN) of Xiphinema americanum mostly overlapping NAD5 (https://arxiv.org/pdf/1001.3813)
- Label and strand errors listed in the same preprint: tRNA-Ser(AGN) of Aphrocallistes vastus annotated as tRNA-Arg in GenBank; tRNA-Ser(AGN) of Albinaria caerulea annotated as complement although it should not be; tRNA-Gln of Varroa destructor given as c(12127..12861) where c(12061..12127) is meant; tRNA-Ser(AGN) of Schizaphis graminum mis-annotated in RefSeq (https://arxiv.org/pdf/1001.3813)
- Drosophila: five RefSeq tRNAs (Arg, Asn, Thr, Pro, Val) were too short to fold and were corrected by RNA-seq; two antisense tRNAs (ThrAS, ProAS) carry 3' CCA (doi:10.1101/2021.06.05.447193)
- RefSeq tRNAs were partly added or checked by NCBI with tRNAscan-SE for microbial and organelle genomes (additions 3679 tRNA and 751 rRNA, deletions 47 tRNA and 4 rRNA, strand corrections 115 tRNA and 103 rRNA, counts not separated by organelle), so some RefSeq mt-tRNA boundaries may be scanner artefacts (doi:10.1093/nar/gkn721)
- MitoZ never reconciles tRNA and CDS overlap (tRNA calls come from MiTFi): an ND1 call can cover a whole trnS (knowledge/tools/mitfi.md:45)
- MITOS2 allows overlaps with `-overlap 50` and the pipeline has no CDS/rRNA overlap filter for tRNA-only runs (knowledge/tools/mitos2.md:68) (knowledge/tools/mitos2.md:84)
