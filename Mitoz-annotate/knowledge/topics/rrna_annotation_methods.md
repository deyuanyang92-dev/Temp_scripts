### 1.3 Method: how rrnS ends were decided (they are inputs, not outputs, of the structure work)
- Data: 27,772 vertebrate mitogenome records from NCBI, cleaned and re-annotated (n = 5,593 re-annotated) with the authors' unpublished NCBI2GO pipeline into 22,179 standardised records with rrnS coordinates parsed from the GenBank FEATURES table [paper] (https://www.arrogantgenome.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/2025-Ver12S.pdf)
- Four-tier validation of rrnS demarcation: (1) co-localisation signature with tRNA-Phe (F) and tRNA-Val (V); (2) cross-validation between MITOS and GenBank annotations; (3) taxon-specific length thresholds (>= 900 bp teleosts, >= 800 bp other vertebrates); (4) exclusion of control-region-proximal sequences; 279 anomalous records were removed after manual inspection, leaving 21,900 high-confidence rrnS, then 800-1300 bp and sequence-identity de-duplication gave 14,048 [paper] (https://www.arrogantgenome.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/2025-Ver12S.pdf)
- The paper states the problem we have: "Gene boundary ambiguity remains a persistent challenge in mitochondrial genome annotation"; GenBank metadata "frequently contain inconsistent annotations, including erroneous start/end coordinates and incomplete 5' or 3' termini, particularly for sequences flanked by the control region (CR) or Mitochondrial 16S rRNA gene" [paper] (https://www.arrogantgenome.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/2025-Ver12S.pdf)
- Structure prediction: automated Python pipeline around SSU-align v0.1.1 (`-gapthresh 0.8 -enone`; the repository README says to run `ssu-build` on a template .stk first, i.e. a custom CM); "phylogenetically stratified iterative template refinement": initial template from mammalian CRW consensus structures, MARNA consensus (gap penalty 5, conservation weight 0.8), avian CRW templates for >1100 bp results, manually curated homolog templates for <850 bp results; quality control by length and by bpRNA domain segmentation; manual check in forna [paper] (https://www.arrogantgenome.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/2025-Ver12S.pdf) (https://github.com/lifengxia0110-lab/ssu-predict12S)
- Result: 21,185 rrnS structures (21,058 in the Introduction of the same paper) grouped into nine types (A-I); Type A (95 %) matches the human 12S architecture, types B-I lose H122, H722, H821 or H1113 (Type D n = 556, Type B n = 291 most frequent); models use E. coli SSU rRNA (J01695) helix numbering; bpRNA decomposition gives 52 elements (31 stems, 21 hairpin loops) and 83 substructures with consistency indices (>= 95 % and >= 85 % classes); bpRNA elements H1, H6, H8, seg6 and seg9 were >= 95 % consistent across taxa [paper] (https://www.arrogantgenome.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/2025-Ver12S.pdf)
- Boundary finding used as evidence of annotation error: H9 loss in Viperidae, Testudinidae, Agamidae and Crocodylidae was caused by a mis-annotated rrnS 5' end; re-annotation found a tRNA-Phe sequence (identity >= 98 %) about 300 bp upstream of the annotated 5' end, and folding "the entire genomic region encompassing both F and mt 12S rRNA" recovered the missing stem-loop [paper] (https://www.arrogantgenome.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/2025-Ver12S.pdf)
- Recommendations: PacBio HiFi data; QC that integrates structural conservation checks (H1-H8 stem verification) and tRNA/rRNA co-localisation; annotation standards should include secondary-structure parameters [paper] (https://www.arrogantgenome.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/2025-Ver12S.pdf)
- No explicit rule such as "rrnS starts at stem H1 5' strand" or a terminal sequence motif is given; ends come from flanking tRNAs, MITOS and GenBank, and structure is used afterwards as a consistency check [inference from the Methods text] (https://www.arrogantgenome.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/2025-Ver12S.pdf)

### 1.5 Companion work from the same group
- Zhou Y, Gong L, Niu G, Shi H, Li X, Gutell R, Wei M. Secondary Structure Diversity of the Mitochondrial Small-Subunit rRNA in Porifera. bioRxiv 2026-08-30, doi:10.64898/2026.08.28.747467 (Jiangxi Normal University, with R. Gutell) [paper] (doi:10.64898/2026.08.28.747467)
- Method: nine conserved structural anchors (stems H9, H367, H500, H769, H885, H921, H944, H1506 and loop H673, E. coli numbering) fixed "to establish homologous boundaries", template-guided modelling "broadly following" Li et al. 2025, bpRNA decomposition, LocARNA consensus for variable regions; of 229 modelled sponge rRNAs, 3 were dropped for incomplete 5' or 3' termini [paper] (https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.08.28.747467v1.full.pdf)
- The anchors delimit internal variable regions; the preprint gives no rule that sets the gene ends [paper] (https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.08.28.747467v1.full.pdf)

### 1.6 Where "12S three domains, 16S six domains" comes from
- Pentatomoidea mitogenomes: "The secondary structure of rrnL consists of six structural domains (domain III is absent in arthropods) and 44 helices, whereas the secondary structure of rrnS contains three domains and 26 helices"; the same pattern is used for Drosophila, Apis, Manduca and other hemipterans; in heteropteran 12S, domain III is more conserved than domains I-II [paper] (https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC4469028/) (doi:10.1371/journal.pone.0055381)
- Aphid example: H2735 is "the last stem-loop of 16S rRNA", variable in size between insects; helix 47 of 12S is variable but its terminal portion is conserved [paper] (doi:10.1371/journal.pone.0077511)
- Heteropteran example: the 12S 5' end is "a long, unpaired sequence followed by a pseudoknot formed by 5 bp stem H9 and the 5' portion of stem H17"; the authors "assumed" the rRNA ends extend to the flanking genes because DNA sequencing alone cannot fix them [paper] (doi:10.1371/journal.pone.0055381)
- Domain III of animal 12S rRNA has conserved sequence motifs usable for alignment: 184 invertebrate and vertebrate taxa (Hickson et al. 1996); bird 12S has four domains, 40 stems and 423 invariant positions (de los Monteros 2003) [paper] (doi:10.1093/oxfordjournals.molbev.a025552) (doi:10.1080/1042517031000149066)

## 2. Other methods that set or check rRNA ends

### 2.1 Processing biology: the tRNA punctuation model
- Ojala, Montoya and Attardi 1981: in human mtDNA the heavy-strand rRNA, mRNA and tRNA sequences form a continuum; the primary transcript is cut "immediately before and after a tRNA sequence", so the 3' end of each mRNA is contiguous with a tRNA gene or another mRNA [paper] (doi:10.1038/290470a0)
- Human NC_012920.1 follows it exactly: trnF 577-647, rrnS 648-1601, trnV 1602-1670, rrnL 1671-3229, trnL1 3230-3304, then nad1 3307-4262 (no gap between rRNAs and tRNAs); rrnS 5' starts AATAGGTTTGGTCC..., rrnL 5' starts GCTAAACCTAGCCCC... [our run] (knowledge/experiments/rrna_ends/human_NC_012920_flank.txt:1-8)
- Drosophila melanogaster mature transcripts (5'/3' RACE, circularised RT-PCR): the model "accurately predicts the 3' ends of most mRNA and rRNA molecules, even where there are non-coding residues present"; non-coding residues at the 5' end are "evidently removed during RNA processing" [paper] (doi:10.1016/j.gene.2009.06.006)
- Human transcriptome with directional deep sequencing and parallel analysis of RNA ends "precisely resolve[s] transcript processing and maturation events"; usable as experimental truth for human rRNA ends, the rRNA-specific end positions were not extracted by us (未确证) (doi:10.1016/j.cell.2011.06.051)
- Consequence [inference]: ends next to a tRNA are set by tRNA excision (strong prior to snap to the tRNA), whereas an end next to the control region (insect rrnS 5') or next to a protein gene (cnidarians, nematode rrnL 3') has no such anchor (doi:10.1038/290470a0) (doi:10.1016/j.gene.2009.06.006)

### 2.2 Annotation practice and tools
- Community convention: "We assumed that the rRNA genes ... start and end at the boundaries of the flanking genes, as is the case in most other insect mt-genomes"; sawfly work: "The precise ends of rRNA genes were predicted from the boundaries of the neighboring tRNA genes" [paper] (doi:10.1038/srep35175) (doi:10.3390/insects12060495)
- NCBI's organelle submission page gives only the count (13 CDS, 22 tRNAs, 2 rRNAs) and "Check the strandedness of the tRNAS and rRNAs"; it states no rule for rRNA ends, so the GenBank "fill between tRNAs" convention is community practice, not a documented requirement (page read 2026-10-21) [our run] (https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/genbank/organelle_submit/)
- MitoAnnotator (fish): the tRNA punctuation model is adopted "as a principal criterion" (rRNA ends = flanking tRNA ends, no gaps), but when it gives an rRNA longer than 1,000 bp (12S) or 1,850 bp (16S) the BLASTN hit is used directly, because "it is intrinsically difficult to determine rRNA gene structures from genomic sequences alone; transcribed rRNAs must be directly sequenced"; tRNA ends include the +1 discriminator base [paper] (doi:10.1093/molbev/mst141)
- MITOS (v1) finds rRNAs with curated Infernal covariance models, the 12S/16S CMs coming from extensively manually curated alignments (MitoZ paper); MITOS2 runs them in glocal mode (DeGeCI paper); the MITOS authors note RefSeq RNA-gene annotation is "plagued by misassignments ... missing as well as false positive annotations in particular for the RNA genes" [paper] (doi:10.1016/j.ympev.2012.08.023) (doi:10.1093/nar/gkz173) (doi:10.3389/fgene.2023.1250907)
- MITOS2 paper: no new rRNA method; the 2019 update concerns PCG start/stop only [paper] (doi:10.1093/nar/gkz833)
- DeGeCI (2023, reference-graph annotator): on RefSeq 89 test genomes the share of rRNA predictions with both ends within 5 nt of RefSeq is 78 % (DeGeCI) vs 70 % (MITOS2), within 10 nt 80 % vs 79 %, within 25 nt 88 % vs 90 %, within 50 nt 94 % vs 96 %; for tRNAs 96 %/99 % and PCGs 85 %/90 % within 5 nt, so rRNA ends are the weakest class for both tools; reference database RefSeq 89, whether each query genome was excluded from its own reference was not checked (未确证) [paper] (doi:10.3389/fgene.2023.1250907)
- MitoFinder: rRNAs located by `blastn` against reference rRNAs (`-word_size 8 -perc_identity 50`), 12S missed in 29 and 16S in 10 of the ant libraries tested [code] [paper] (knowledge/tools/mitofinder.md:26) (knowledge/tools/mitofinder.md:61)
- MFannot: rns/rnl by HMM wrappers (rnl ends only within about +-50 nt); its paper states rns/rnl are not identified in Bilateria [code] [paper] (knowledge/tools/mfannot.md:38)
- MiTFi is tRNA-only (CMs of 22 metazoan tRNAs, applied to 1,876 RefSeq genomes); ARWEN/ARAGORN detect tRNA and tmRNA genes only; these are the tRNA anchors that rRNA snapping depends on [paper] (doi:10.1093/nar/gkr1131) (doi:10.1093/nar/gkh152)
- No 2015-2026 tool whose purpose is refining rRNA ends was found in Europe PMC title/abstract searches for rRNA + mitochondrial + boundary/annotation terms (hits were genome papers and PCG tools), nor on the MITOS and MitoZ issue trackers (searches "rRNA", "rrn", "12S" on 2026-10-21); a missed tool is possible (未确证) (https://gitlab.com/Bernt/MITOS/-/issues)

### 2.3 Structure resources and checks
- Rfam release 15.1 (2026-01-16) has no metazoan mitochondrial SSU or LSU family; keyword "mito" returns only trypanosomatid mt SSU/LSU, mt-tmRNA, mtPerm-5S and mtDNA_ssA, so there is no Rfam CM to borrow [our run] (https://rfam.org/search/keyword?query=mito&output=json)
- SSU-align ships only archaeal, bacterial and eukaryotic SSU models; CRW has mitochondrial SSU alignments but they were not built into the default CMs, so mt-12S needs `ssu-build` from a template alignment (as Li et al. did) [paper] (http://eddylab.org/software/ssu-align/Userguide.pdf)

## 3. Conserved end motifs and end contexts per taxon

All numbers below are from the RefSeq panel (n = 16 per clade, 125 insects, 96 vertebrates per gene) or the dev truth; "gap 0" = the NCBI rRNA end is contiguous with the neighbouring annotated feature.

### 3.1 What the neighbours are (the context an end rule can use)
- Dev set, all 186 NCBI rRNAs: both ends contiguous with the neighbouring features in 94 (51 %); both ends within 10 nt in 119 (64 %); per end 60-64 % are gap 0 and 7-14 % overlap the neighbour [our run] (knowledge/experiments/rrna_ends/truth_flank_summary_dev.txt:2-4) (knowledge/experiments/rrna_ends/truth_flank_dev.txt:3-6)
- By neighbour type (dev, 372 ends): tRNA neighbours are gap 0 in 191 of 276 (69 %), overlap in 29 (10 %), gap 1-50 in 31, 51-300 in 10, >300 in 15; CDS neighbours are gap 0 in only 18 of 66 (27 %), overlap in 6, gap 1-50 in 19, 51-300 in 15, >300 in 8 [our run] (knowledge/experiments/rrna_ends/truth_flank_summary_dev.txt:6-8)
- Vertebrates (panel, 96): rrnS 5' contiguous with trnF in 79, overlapping the tRNA in 12, gap 1-10 in 5; rrnS 3' contiguous with trnV in 74, 6 with 1-10 nt, 16 with no trnV annotated and rrnL as nearest feature at > 50 nt; rrnL 5' contiguous with trnV in 70, rrnL 3' contiguous with trnL1 in 78 [our run] (knowledge/experiments/rrna_ends/panel_neighbours_refseq.txt:141-154)
- Insects (panel, 125): rrnL 5' contiguous with trnV in 80 (64 %), 1-10 nt in 14, 11-50 nt in 13, >50 nt in 18; rrnL 3' contiguous with trnL1 in 78, and in 20 the 3' neighbour is a CDS (ND1 in most; 12 with an 11-50 nt gap, trnL1 absent or displaced); rrnS 3' contiguous with trnV in 76, 1-10 nt in 26 (15 of them trnV(tac)) [our run] (knowledge/experiments/rrna_ends/panel_neighbours_refseq.txt:127-138)
- Insect rrnS 5' (control-region side): 66 of 125 are "contiguous" and 45 overlap the next annotated feature, but the neighbour there is an A+T-rich/D-loop/misc feature (73 of 125) that submitters place after the rRNA, so contiguity is by construction and carries no information on where the rRNA really starts; 11 of the 45 overlaps are with trnI(gat) [our run] [inference] (knowledge/experiments/rrna_ends/panel_neighbours_refseq.txt:135-136)
- Cnidaria have no tRNA anchor: rrnS neighbours are CDS in 16 of 16 and contiguous in only 1; rrnL 3' neighbour is CDS in 15 of 16 (gap >50 in 9, overlapping COX1 in 2); dev: 0 of 12 Cnidaria and 0 of 6 Placozoa rRNAs have both ends contiguous, against 10 of 12 Chordata and 9 of 12 Platyhelminthes [our run] (knowledge/experiments/rrna_ends/panel_neighbours_refseq.txt:29-42) (knowledge/experiments/rrna_ends/truth_flank_summary_dev.txt:16-33)
- Nematode rrnL 3' is contiguous with ND3 in 13 of 16 (neighbour CDS), rrnS 5' with a tRNA (Glu, Thr) in 11 of 16 [our run] (knowledge/experiments/rrna_ends/panel_neighbours_refseq.txt:85-98)

### 3.2 Terminal sequence motifs (weak)
- Vertebrate rrnS 5' end (n = 96): modal first ten nucleotides CAAAGGTTTG with modal-base frequencies 0.50, 0.89, 0.88, 0.81, 0.75, 0.49, 0.61, 0.88, 0.85, 0.78; human is AATAGGTTTGG; the AAAG...TTTG core is probably the 5' strand of the first helix (helix assignment not checked, 未确证) [our run] (knowledge/experiments/rrna_ends/panel_neighbours_refseq.txt:153)
- Insect rrnS 5' end (n = 125): modal first ten TTAAAGTTTT, frequencies 0.46, 0.46, 0.64, 0.66, 0.69, 0.47, 0.77, 0.79, 0.78, 0.65, i.e. the same AAAG(T)TTT core at positions 3-10; Diptera 5-mer ATAAA in 6 of 16, Testudines CAAAG in 13 of 16, Aves AAAAG in 12 of 16 [our run] (knowledge/experiments/rrna_ends/panel_neighbours_refseq.txt:139) (knowledge/experiments/rrna_ends/panel_end_kmers_refseq.txt:1-46)
- Other ends (rrnS 3', rrnL 5'/3', and all clades other than insects/vertebrates) show no motif beyond base composition: modal terminal 5-mers cover 6-38 % of a clade, and the high per-position frequencies are poly-A/T runs (e.g. Chelicerata rrnS 3' TTT 0.75-0.94, Nematoda rrnL 5' TAAATTTTTT 0.56-0.81) [our run] (knowledge/experiments/rrna_ends/panel_end_kmers_refseq.txt:1-46)
- Reason [inference]: terminal nucleotides are in low-complexity A+T stretches and in helices whose sequence is free to covary; a terminal-sequence rule would need a CM, not a consensus string (no direct test of the cause, 未确证) (knowledge/experiments/rrna_ends/panel_end_kmers_refseq.txt:1-46)

### 3.3 Where RefSeq ends fall on the MITOS CMs (model columns)
- MITOS2 rrnS (894 columns, built from 7,991 sequences): the RefSeq start maps to model column <= 3 in 99 % of vertebrates (93 of 94), 66 % of insects (81 of 123), 100 % of echinoderms, 93 % of polychaetes, 71 % of flatworms, but 0 of 16 chelicerates and 0 of 16 nematodes (median start column 83 and 78.5); the end maps to column >= 891 in 97 % of vertebrates, 98 % of insects, 100 % of crustaceans, but 12 % of chelicerates [our run] (knowledge/experiments/rrna_ends/panel_model_columns_refseq.txt:1-12)

## 4. What MITOS2 and MitoZ do at rRNA ends, and where they fail

### 4.1 MitoZ 3.6
- Two Infernal 1.1.1 `cmsearch -g` runs (glocal) with `v1.1_12snew.cm` (CLEN 952) and `v1.1_16snew.cm` (CLEN 1630); only if the global tblout has no hit is a local search run; the same two models for every clade [code] (mitoz/annotate/annotation.py:322-340) (mitoz/annotate/annotation.py:357-376)
- The models are the MITOS v1 models, DATE 2010-02-09, built from alignments named `12snew_curated.47` and `16snew_curated.81`, converted with `cmconvert` to Infernal 1.1 format [code] (mitoz/profiles/rRNA_CM/README.txt:1-12) (mitoz/profiles/rRNA_CM/v1.1_12snew.cm:1-11)
- Hit filter: only `!` hits (inclusion E <= 0.01), aligned length > 100 nt, a second hit accepted only if >= 5,000 nt from the first; the coordinates are copied from columns 8-9 of the tblout to the feature table with no trimming against tRNAs and no look at neighbours [code] (mitoz/annotate/script/rRNA_ft.py:71-85) (mitoz/annotate/script/rRNA_ft.py:97-104)
- Weakness 1, whole-model ends: glocal alignment spans model columns 1..CLEN in 77 of 77 (12S) and 79 of 81 (16S) hits, so the ends are the model's termini, not the gene's: 16S median overshoot +33 nt (5') and +24 nt (3') relative to NCBI, exact ends 4 and 5 of 81, within 3 nt 9 and 10; 12S exact ends 20 and 24 of 77 [our run] (knowledge/experiments/rrna_ends/cm_glocal_ends_dev.txt:1-4)
- Weakness 2, wrong-clade model: the 2010 models are poorly matched to non-vertebrates (insect 12S start column median 66, 16S start 22, section 3.3), and 12S 5' overshoots by more than 50 nt in 21 of 77 genomes [our run] (knowledge/experiments/rrna_ends/cm_end_offset_hist_dev.txt:3)
- Weakness 3, overlap with tRNAs: in an earlier local test the 16S hit overlapped trnL(uag) by 23 nt and trnV by 2 nt; `mito_curate.py` R1 exists for this [our run] (knowledge/tools/mitoz.md:36) (mito_curate.py:279-319)
- Whole-pipeline numbers on the dev set (NCBI truth): MitoZ raw rrnS both ends exact 8 of 41 (within 3 nt at 5': 25, 3': 24), rrnL both exact 2 of 40 (median +22.5 / +20.5 nt); our MitoM output in the dev evaluation both exact 11 of 41 (rrnS) and 11 of 40 (rrnL), 22 in total, median error 0 (this equals the "trim" count in the `mito_curate.py` help text; the "fill(300)" count of 33 was measured separately) [our run] (knowledge/experiments/rrna_ends/tool_end_errors_dev.txt:6-13)
- Also unresolved in MitoZ itself: longer-than-published 12S and doubled/split 12S when the bundled `cmsearch.linux` cannot run (https://github.com/linzhi2013/MitoZ/issues/146) (https://github.com/linzhi2013/MitoZ/issues/187)

### 4.2 MITOS2
- Models: `refseq89m/ncRNA/rrnS.cm` (CLEN 894, NSEQ 7,991, Infernal 1.1.2, built 2018-08-27) and `rrnL.cm` (CLEN 1537, NSEQ 7,902), i.e. rebuilt from RefSeq 89 and 6 % shorter than the MITOS1 models (952 and 1630 columns); MAP annotation yes [code] (/opt/mitos_refs/refseq89m/ncRNA/rrnS.cm:1-20) (/opt/mitos_refs/refseq89m/ncRNA/rrnL.cm:1-20)
- Search: one `cmsearch` per `rrn*.cm` with `-E <evalue>`, `-g` for the glocal pass and MiTFi called with `-ribosomal -overlap <rrnaovl>`; sensitive mode uses `--nohmm` for rRNAs; a local pass (`gl=False`) runs only for an rRNA that has no glocal result [code] (mitos/mitfi/__init__.py:101-160) (mitos/scripts/runmitos.py:594-612)
- Conflict handling: candidates ordered by E-value; a tRNA overlapping an rRNA is tolerated up to tRNA length - 1 (round 1: a tRNA with E > 1e-3 overlapping an rRNA is removed); CLI `--maxrrnaovl` default 50 [code] (mitos/mergefeature/__init__.py:22-35) (mitos/mergefeature/__init__.py:81-90) (knowledge/tools/mitos2.md:51)
- `cmrealign` re-aligns a finished rRNA to the model only to obtain a structure string; it does not move ends [code] (mitos/mitfi/__init__.py:232-285)
- No rRNA end optimisation (compare the PCG start/stop model, topics/pcg_boundary_methods.md): ends are the cmsearch/MiTFi hit coordinates [inference from the code path] (mitos/mitfi/__init__.py:101-160)
- Measured end accuracy of the MITOS2 CMs alone (best glocal hit, no snapping): rrnS 5' exact 26 of 81, 3' exact 24, both within 3 nt 25; rrnL 5' exact 8 of 85, 3' exact 13, both within 3 nt 10 (median +15 / +8 nt) — better than MitoZ's models (rrnS both within 3 nt 19 of 77; rrnL 4 of 81) but far from sufficient [our run] (knowledge/experiments/rrna_ends/cm_glocal_ends_dev.txt:1-8)
- Pipeline-level MITOS2 on the dev set (full runs, includes missed/duplicate hits): rrnS both exact 13 of 85, rrnL 5 of 75, rrnL median 5' -3 nt and 3' +12 nt; 26 rRNAs missed and 21 false positives among 207 calls [our run] (knowledge/experiments/rrna_ends/tool_end_errors_dev.txt:1-5)
- Direction of the 3' error of the rrnS CM: 20 of 81 end 4-10 nt beyond NCBI and 15 more 1-3 nt beyond, consistent with overlap into trnV that trimming removes [our run] [inference] (knowledge/experiments/rrna_ends/cm_end_offset_hist_dev.txt:2)
- MITOS gitlab: open issues on rRNA concern crashes (cmsearch exit -9, memory), not end positions; `cmrealign.str` missing with many Ns (https://gitlab.com/Bernt/MITOS/-/issues/56) (https://gitlab.com/Bernt/MITOS/-/issues/48)

### 4.3 Our curation (`mito_curate.py` R1)
- R1 trims an rRNA so it does not overlap any flanking tRNA/CDS/rRNA, then extends each end to the neighbour when the gap is <= `--max_fill` (default 300 nt); help text records the dev benchmark: both ends exact with trim only 22, fill(300) 33 of 90 rRNAs [code] (mito_curate.py:279-319) (mito_curate.py:412-419)
- Ceiling of the whole "end = neighbour boundary" family: the NCBI records themselves are contiguous at both ends in only 94 of 186 dev rRNAs (51 %) and within +-10 nt in 119 (64 %), so 33 of 90 (37 %) is not far below what any neighbour rule can reach on this truth [our run] [inference] (knowledge/experiments/rrna_ends/truth_flank_dev.txt:1-6)
- Behaviour of the MITOS2-CM end against the truth, by distance to the nearest neighbour boundary (idealised neighbours: the nearest annotated feature outside the NCBI rRNA, 313 ends): CM end inside the neighbour by 1-3 nt: truth = neighbour boundary in 30 of 32; by 4-10: 23 of 30; by 11-30: 25 of 28; by > 30: 44 of 59; CM end in a gap of 1-3 nt: 9 of 13; 4-10: 10 of 19; 11-30: 16 of 25 (vs CM end exact in 3, 4, 3); 31-100: 11 of 33 vs CM end exact 13; > 100: 14 of 46 vs 7 [our run] (knowledge/experiments/rrna_ends/cm_end_vs_neighbour_dev.txt:3-12)
- Raw CM end is within 3 nt of the truth for only 32 % of ends where the truth is contiguous with a neighbour (67 of 212) but for 55-56 % of ends where the truth overlaps the neighbour (19 of 34) or leaves a 1-10 nt gap (11 of 20) [our run] (knowledge/experiments/rrna_ends/cm_end_vs_truth_gap_dev.txt:2-6)

## 5. Ideas to integrate (ranked by evidence per effort)

Idea 1 (most promising, low cost): search rRNAs with the MITOS2 RefSeq-89 CMs instead of the MITOS1 CMs bundled with MitoZ, keep R1 snapping.
- Evidence: (a) raw glocal ends closer to NCBI (rrnL median +15/+8 nt vs +33/+24; both-within-3 rrnS 25 of 81 vs 19 of 77, rrnL 10 of 85 vs 4 of 81), (b) hit found more often (81 vs 77 of rrnS, 85 vs 81 of rrnL), (c) RefSeq insect rrnS starts fit the MITOS2 model terminus (66 % vs 18 %), (d) after snapping with idealised neighbours (fill <= 300) both ends exact 39 of 81 (rrnS) and 41 of 85 (rrnL) vs 33 of 77 and 38 of 81 with the MitoZ models [our run] (knowledge/experiments/rrna_ends/cm_glocal_ends_dev.txt:1-8) (knowledge/experiments/rrna_ends/snap_rule_sim_truth_neighbours_dev.txt:6) (knowledge/experiments/rrna_ends/snap_rule_sim_truth_neighbours_dev.txt:15) (knowledge/experiments/rrna_ends/snap_rule_sim_truth_neighbours_dev.txt:24) (knowledge/experiments/rrna_ends/snap_rule_sim_truth_neighbours_dev.txt:33)
- When the two models' ends disagree by more than 3 nt, the MITOS2 end is within 3 nt of NCBI in 46 % (rrnS, n = 37 ends) vs 11 % for the MitoZ model, and 10 % vs 2 % for rrnL (n = 126), so the MITOS2 model should win a disagreement rather than being averaged with it [our run] (knowledge/experiments/rrna_ends/model_agreement_dev.txt:1-5)
- Needs: `/opt/mitos_refs/refseq89m/ncRNA/rrnS.cm` and `rrnL.cm` (present), Infernal >= 1.1.2 (1.1.5 in the mitos env; whether Infernal 1.1.1 in the mitoz env reads them is 未确证), a glocal pass with local fallback as MITOS2 does; implement as an rRNA re-search before R1 in `mito_curate.py` or as a replacement of MitoZ's rRNA step. Test: run on the dev split, compare exact / within-3 per end against `tool_end_errors_dev.txt` using benchmark/eval_features.py; accept if both-exact rises by >= 5 of 90 and no rRNA is lost. Not measured at full scale: effect with predicted (not idealised) tRNA neighbours; with MitoM's own neighbours (only the dev genomes that have a MitoM output and a hit, 40-43 per model) both-exact was 18 of 42 (MITOS2 rrnS) vs 17 of 41 (MitoZ) and 23 of 43 vs 23 of 40 for rrnL, i.e. no visible difference at that n [our run] (knowledge/experiments/rrna_ends/snap_rule_sim_ours_neighbours_dev.txt:1-36)

Idea 2 (cheap, reporting only): clade length windows and a four-tier QC flag instead of silent output.
- Evidence: Li et al. reject rrnS outside >= 900 bp (teleost) / >= 800 bp (other vertebrates) and 800-1300 bp overall and drop CR-proximal records; the panel gives empirical windows (5th-95th percentile: insect rrnS 733-836, rrnL 1220-1386; vertebrate rrnS 921-982, rrnL 1478-1700; min-max of 14 flatworms: rrnS 712-776) and shows RefSeq itself contains a 56 nt "rrnS" [paper] [our run] (https://www.arrogantgenome.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/2025-Ver12S.pdf) (knowledge/experiments/rrna_ends/panel_rrna_lengths_refseq.txt:1-23)
- Rule: after R1, warn in the curation log (not edit) when the length is outside the clade window, or when R1 moved an end by more than 100 nt (the neighbour boundary is then right in only 30 % of cases, 14 of 46, though still twice as often as the CM end, 7 of 46; at 31-100 nt 33 % vs 39 %). Needs: the length table (from RefSeq, per phylum/class); no new software [inference] (knowledge/experiments/rrna_ends/cm_end_vs_neighbour_dev.txt:3-4)

Idea 3 (benchmark hygiene, before tuning anything): score rRNA ends against a defensible truth.
- Evidence: the truth is contiguous with its neighbours in only 51 % of cases, Li et al. document erroneous start/end coordinates and incomplete termini in GenBank especially next to the control region and 16S, MITOS authors call RefSeq RNA annotation "plagued by misassignments", and in Viperidae, Testudinidae, Agamidae and Crocodylidae a tRNA-Phe was found by re-annotation about 300 bp upstream of the annotated 12S 5' end [paper] [our run] (https://www.arrogantgenome.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/2025-Ver12S.pdf) (doi:10.1016/j.ympev.2012.08.023)
- Rule: report three numbers per end (exact, within 3, within 10) and split by neighbour class (tRNA, CDS, control region); for vertebrates use the 21,900 QC-passed rrnS coordinates of Li et al. (figshare `1_NCBI2GO_table.zip`) as a second truth; add the tier-A (longread/transcriptome) records separately (14 rRNAs in dev, 6 of them contiguous at both ends, too few to tune on). Needs: download 6 MB, map accessions to benchmark rows [inference] (knowledge/experiments/rrna_ends/truth_flank_summary_dev.txt:39-42) (https://figshare.com/projects/Vertebrate-12S/235928)

Idea 4 (needs data we may not have): transcript-end evidence for the ends that have no tRNA anchor.
- Evidence: processing follows tRNA punctuation at 3' ends, but non-coding 5' residues are trimmed during maturation, so the rrnS 5' end next to the control region (insect, 66 % "contiguous" only by construction) cannot be read from genome features; RACE or RNA-seq ends fix it (Drosophila RACE; human directional RNA-seq with parallel analysis of RNA ends) [paper] (doi:10.1016/j.gene.2009.06.006) (doi:10.1016/j.cell.2011.06.051)
- Rule: when RNA-seq reads of the same specimen exist, map them (any splice-unaware aligner) and call rrnS 5' / rrnL 3' as the coverage drop point within 30 nt of the CM/tRNA-snapped end; otherwise leave the end as CM+snap and flag. Needs: reads, aligner, coverage script; untested here, 未确证 as an improvement [inference] (doi:10.1016/j.gene.2009.06.006)

Idea 5 (only if Ideas 1-3 plateau): per-neighbour-type fill rule.
- Evidence: tRNA neighbours are contiguous in 69 % but CDS neighbours in 27 %, and truth equals the neighbour boundary for a CM end 11-30 nt away in 64 % (vs 12 % for the CM end); but replaying R1 with idealised neighbours gave rrnS both-exact 39 of 81 (fill <= 300, any neighbour), 37 (tRNA neighbours only) and 33 (fill <= 30), and rrnL 41, 41 and 41 of 85, i.e. restricting to tRNA neighbours is neutral to slightly negative and lowering the threshold to 30 nt loses 6 for rrnS [our run] (knowledge/experiments/rrna_ends/cm_end_vs_neighbour_dev.txt:5) (knowledge/experiments/rrna_ends/snap_rule_sim_truth_neighbours_dev.txt:3) (knowledge/experiments/rrna_ends/snap_rule_sim_truth_neighbours_dev.txt:6-7) (knowledge/experiments/rrna_ends/snap_rule_sim_truth_neighbours_dev.txt:21-25)
- Conclusion [inference]: keep fill <= 300 for all neighbour types unless a test-split replay says otherwise; any per-type rule should be tested on the test split only after the model choice is frozen, to avoid tuning to the dev set (knowledge/experiments/rrna_ends/snap_rule_sim_truth_neighbours_dev.txt:6-7)

Negative results (do not repeat without a new angle)
- Clade-specific CMs built from the 14-155 panel sequences of the same phylum (aligned to the MITOS2 model, `cmbuild`, E-value parameters copied from the parent model): raw ends no better than the universal MITOS2 model (rrnS both within 3 nt 17 vs 15 of 47, rrnL 5 vs 9) and after snapping equal (rrnS both exact 22-23 vs 22-24; rrnL 20-25 vs 25-26); glocal scoring does not pin the poorly conserved termini even when the model length matches the clade [our run] (knowledge/experiments/rrna_ends/clade_cm_vs_universal_dev.txt:1-12)
- Clipping the universal-CM hit to the clade-median RefSeq model columns (e.g. nematode rrnS start column 78, insect rrnL end column 1500): raw both-exact 6 vs 9 (rrnS) and 2 vs 4 (rrnL); with snapping no better than snapping alone (rrnS 21 vs 22, rrnL 18 vs 26 of 45); the NCBI start/end columns vary too much inside a phylum [our run] (knowledge/experiments/rrna_ends/column_clip_dev.txt:1-15)
- Terminal consensus strings: only vertebrate and insect rrnS 5' ends have a recognisable core (AAAG..TTTG), too weak to place an end within 3 nt (section 3.2) [our run] (knowledge/experiments/rrna_ends/panel_end_kmers_refseq.txt:1-46)
