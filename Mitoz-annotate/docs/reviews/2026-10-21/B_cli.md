## Declined to judge

- The full `docker build` of the image (27 min, 11 GB per benchmark/sets/install_2026-10.tsv) was not run; finding 1 rests on the PATH/Biopython probe described there, not on a built mitom image.
- Correctness of NCBI lineages for every phylum in METAZOAN_PHYLA / PHYLUM_CLADE (e.g. whether current NCBI places Sipuncula/Echiura under Annelida, Acanthocephala under Rotifera, how Dicyemida/Orthonectida/Rhombozoa are ranked): I could not check NCBI Taxonomy; names present in METAZOAN_PHYLA looked plausible, but a lineage that contains both Rotifera and Acanthocephala would pick the first one in lineage order and records without the outer name would count as a second phylum [unconfirmed].
- Whether `mito_env.find("nhmmer")` may pick MitoZ's HMMER 3.1b2 binary (conda scan order) on a fresh install, and whether it reads the HMMER 3.4 models (the mitos-env 3.4 nhmmer does, tested). Not tested with 3.1b2.
- `--scheduler_backend snakemake` with an empty task list (finding 11), and `conda env create -y` fallback behaviour across conda versions (install.sh:88).
- MitoZ's own clade/genetic-code table (MITOZ_CODES) and the PHYLUM_CODE defaults against NCBI genetic-code assignments per taxon; the quality of the Animal/extended-clade protein databases (benchmark topic, not CLI).
- The scientific adequacy of reorient_check as a predictor that shifted round 1 equals a round-2 re-annotation (only 6 dev records of A/B evidence; identical features reported there).
- Behaviour on macOS/Apple Silicon (install.sh Rosetta branch): no such machine available.
